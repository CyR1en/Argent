# 2 · Negative / Print

One frame cut down the middle: the left half is the colour negative, orange mask and all, where the subject is thin and pale; the right half is the print, silver paper where the same subject comes out dark. The disc is only whole when read across the seam, which is what Argent models: a negative, then a print.

Layers: three front to back, `1-negative-disc.svg`, `2-print.svg`, `3-negative.svg`, one group each so the disc floats above the negative; fill from `background.txt`. Liquid Glass on for all; translucency off on the disc, about 20 percent on the two halves; neutral shadow. The print's disc is a true cut-out, so the background is the print's dark. The SVGs and the assembled Icon Composer document are the shipped icon, [`App/Argent.icon`](../../../../App/Argent.icon) (layers in its `Assets/`); `preview.svg` is a flat reference without the glass.
