Models included in this pack

1. S3OD-sod (s3od-sod-sq1024-f16.aimodel) — finds the subject
   - Code (KupynOrest/s3od): MIT License, Copyright (c) 2024 S3OD Team
     (S3OD-LICENSE-MIT.txt)
   - Weights (okupyn/s3od-sod): S3OD-sod-model-card.md; Built with DINOv3,

   - Training data: includes images generated with FLUX.1 Krea-dev under the
     FLUX.1 [dev] Non-Commercial License (FLUX1-Krea-dev-LICENSE.md), along
     with DIS5K, HR-SOD, UHRSOD, and DUTS

2. EoMT-DINOv3-L (eomt-sq768-f16.aimodel) — checks for a scene with no subject,
   and for sky reflected in water
   - Code (tue-mps/eomt): MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025 Mobile Perception
     Systems Lab at TU/e (EoMT-LICENSE-MIT.txt)
   - Weights (tue-mps/eomt-dinov3-ade-semantic-large-512): fine-tuned from
     DINOv3 ViT-L/16; Built with DINOv3, distributed under Meta's DINOv3
     License (DINOv3-LICENSE.md)
   - Training data (ADE20K): images for non-commercial research and educational
     use; annotations under BSD-3-Clause, Copyright 2019 MIT CSAIL
     (ADE20K-terms.txt)

3. ViTMatte-S, Composition-1k (vitmatte-s-c1k-sq1024-f16.aimodel) — draws soft
   edges in low light
   - Code (hustvl/ViTMatte): MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023 Hust Vision Lab
     (ViTMatte-LICENSE-MIT.txt)
   - Weights (hustvl/vitmatte-small-composition-1k):
     ViTMatte-S-Composition1k-model-card.md; DINO v1 ViT-S/16 backbone
     (Apache-2.0)
   - Training data (Composition-1k, Adobe Deep Image Matting dataset): for
     non-commercial use and distribution only; may not be sold.
     Citation: Ning Xu, Brian Price, Scott Cohen and Thomas Huang. Deep Image
     Matting. CVPR 2017.

4. Depth Anything 3 Mono-Large (da3-sky-sq756-f16.aimodel) — finds the sky
   - Code (ByteDance-Seed/Depth-Anything-3) and weights
